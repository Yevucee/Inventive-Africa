The [Innovation Prize for Africa](http://www.innovationprizeforafrica.org/) (IPA) hosted its first ever Twitter chat on Wednesday, 23 September 2015, entitled: _[Why Innovation Competitions are Important in Africa](https://twitter.com/search?q=%23IPA2016&src=typd)_. The Twitter chat featured IPA Director, Pauline Mujawamariya (@PaulineM4), CEO of The Innovation Hub in South Africa, Mclean Sibanda (@mcleansibanda) and IPA 2015 prize winner, Alex Muriu (@hellomuriu). IPA calls itself "a landmark program of the [African Innovation Foundation](http://www.africaninnovation.org/) whose purpose is to increase the prosperity of Africans through catalysing the innovation spirit in Africa."
